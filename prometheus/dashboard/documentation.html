Prometheus is an autonomous multi-agent platform designed for software engineering fleets. Operating under the guiding principle of "Propose, Don't Impose", it ingests telemetry across GitHub, Jira Cloud, and Slack, synthesizes root-cause blocker dependency graphs, and prepares Human-in-the-Loop action cards.
